Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 August 2026  ·  Last updated: 25 August 2026
This policy explains what data the PortRai app collects, why we collect it, and how you can control it. We believe in transparency and data minimisation.

1. Who We Are

PortRai ("we", "our", "Service Provider") operates the PortRai mobile application ("Application"). For questions about this policy, contact us at mayank271993@gmail.com.

2. Data We Collect

The table below lists every category of personal data the Application collects, the source, and the purpose.

Category Data collected Source Purpose
Usage analytics Screen views, in-app events, session duration, approximate location (country/city), device model, OS version Google Analytics for Firebase Understand how the app is used and improve it
Crash & diagnostic data Stack traces, device state at crash time, app version Firebase Crashlytics Detect and fix bugs
App configuration Feature-flag values fetched remotely Firebase Remote Config Deliver app configuration without an update
Portfolio content Read-only portfolio data (projects, experience, profile) Cloud Firestore / Firebase Storage Display app content — no user data is written

The Application does not collect precise GPS location, contacts, camera, or microphone data.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To display portfolio content fetched from Cloud Firestore.
  • To monitor performance, detect crashes, and fix bugs.
  • To understand feature usage and prioritise improvements.
  • To deliver the correct app configuration via Remote Config.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

The Application relies on the following third-party services. Each operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Cloud Firestore & Firebase Storage
  • Firebase Remote Config & App Check

All Firebase services are provided by Google LLC and are covered by Google's Data Processing Addendum.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your data. We may share it only in the following circumstances:

  • Legal obligation: When required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Protection of rights: To protect the safety, rights, or property of the Service Provider or users.
  • Service providers: With sub-processors (such as Firebase) who assist us in operating the Application, under strict data-processing agreements.
  • Business transfer: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice provided to affected users.

6. Data Retention

The Application does not require an account and does not store any personally identifiable information on our servers. Analytics and crash-report data are retained for up to 14 months by default in Firebase, after which they are automatically deleted or anonymised.

To request removal of any data associated with your device, contact us at mayank271993@gmail.com. We will process the request within 30 days.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request erasure of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection / restriction: Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Opt-out of analytics: You can disable Firebase Analytics collection in your device's app settings or by uninstalling the Application.

To exercise any of these rights, email mayank271993@gmail.com.

8. Security

We implement technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest for all Firebase data.
  • Firebase App Check to prevent unauthorised API access.
  • Firestore security rules that enforce per-user data access.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU/EEA where applicable). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data without parental consent, please contact us immediately and we will delete the data promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will notify you via an in-app notice or email. Continued use of the Application after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
